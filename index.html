about me

I am a human-centered technology researcher studying how people make consequential and subjective decisions when AI becomes part of the process, especially in groups, where people interpret both AI and one another.

I use experiments, interviews, surveys, and mixed-methods analysis to understand behavior and translate findings into design guidance for more supportive AI systems. My background in design, HCI, and emerging technology helps me approach these questions as both behavioral patterns and design problems.
