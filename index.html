about me

I study how people make consequential and subjective decisions when AI becomes part of the process. I am especially interested in group settings, where people are not only interpreting AI but also each other. As people work through a decision, social influence changes what they trust, whose judgment carries more weight, and how different positions are negotiated before they settle on an answer. My work looks at these dynamics closely and asks why people rely on AI in some moments, rely on each other in others, and how those choices shape the final outcome. My background in design, human computer interaction, and emerging technology gives me a way to look at these questions as both behavioral patterns and design problems, asking not just how people decide alongside AI, but how these systems could be built to support that better.
